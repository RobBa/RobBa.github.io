About me

Hi, I'm Robert, currently pursuing a PhD degree at the TU Delft in the Netherlands and part of the cyber-analytics lab. We collaboratively try to model discrete event systems such as networks and software systems to infer visual representations of the systems. To this means we are developing and enhancing the Flexfringe tool, which we use to build automata from system traces.

Apart from our research my interests lie in general in applied statistics, machine learning, algorithms, and well written code. My main languages are C++ and Python. In my free time I like to do sports, engage with music, and learn more about the world.
